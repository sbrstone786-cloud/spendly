# Spendly — Free Subscription Tracker & Cost Calculator

**Live site:** https://spendly-beryl-three.vercel.app

Spendly is a free tool that shows what your subscriptions really cost — monthly and yearly — and helps you cancel, pause, or keep the ones worth it. No signup, no bank linking: your data stays in your browser.

## Free tools

- [Subscription Cost Calculator](https://spendly-beryl-three.vercel.app/subscription-cost-calculator) — add up every subscription in one place
- [FIRE Calculator](https://spendly-beryl-three.vercel.app/fire-calculator) — when can you retire, and how much do you need?
- [Coast FIRE Calculator](https://spendly-beryl-three.vercel.app/coast-fire-calculator) — how much you need saved now to coast to retirement
- [Dashboard](https://spendly-beryl-three.vercel.app/dashboard) — track renewals and totals
